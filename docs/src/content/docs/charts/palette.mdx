Every chart colour does one job. Pick the job first, then the colour.

| What the colour says | Tokens | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Which one | `--c-chart-1` to `8` | Fixed order, never cycled. A ninth series folds into Other. |
| This one matters | `--c-chart-1`, `--c-chart-neutral` | Highlight it, grey the rest. |
| How much | `--c-seq-*`, `--c-seq2-*` | One hue. Step 1 means little, in both themes. |
| Above or below | `--c-div-*` | Two hues around a near-grey midpoint. |
| Good or bad | `--c-status-*` | Reserved. Always with an icon and a label. |
| Where each day of a record stands | `--c-state-*` | Complete, waiting, set aside, missing. For [day strips](/components/day-strips/). |

## Categorical

Eight hues in a fixed order, the same hue per slot in both themes, so a series keeps its colour when the theme changes. Orange and teal lead, the pair the sites already use: slot 1 is the orange each theme uses, and slot 2 is the same teal as the tags. Chroma stays close to 0.11, so nothing shouts over the orange, and lightness alternates slot by slot, so neighbours stay apart in greyscale.

The ratio is against the sunken plate, the hardest surface in light mode. Results for every surface are on the [Checks](/checks/) page.

## Diverging

Teal below the baseline, orange above it. The midpoint has almost no chroma, because a peach midpoint would read as "a little above".

## Status

Reserved meaning, never "series 4". Good and critical differ in lightness as well as hue, because at the same lightness they collapse for colour-blind readers.

## Record states

The state of each day in a long record. Complete is soft, because it is most of what you see. Missing is the ink, the one that asks for something. Set aside is the neutral grey, drawn in stripes. Waiting recedes: lighter than complete on light, darker on dark, so it is never taken for complete, also by colour-blind readers.

## Where it stops working

- **Eight colours can't all stay apart for colour-blind readers.** Some pairs that aren't neighbours get close (see Checks for the numbers). Stacks and lines only have neighbours touching, so they can use all eight. Scatter plots, maps and small multiples stop at three.
- **Labels inside a fill pass WCAG but mostly score low on APCA.** So keep them large, or put them outside the mark.
- **Deep teal sits under the usual chroma floor.** Slot 2 in light reaches 0.08, where the guideline asks for 0.10, because sRGB can't go further at that lightness. It stays far from the orange, which is what the floor protects.
- **Dark mode goes above the usual lightness limit.** The common guideline stops at 0.67. The brand orange sits at 0.69, and I'd rather keep the real orange, so the checks allow up to 0.76.
- **Complete and waiting are soft on purpose,** under 3:1 against the card. They are most of a strip, and the tooltip and a table carry the detail. Missing and set aside reach 3:1.
- **The simulations are models.** None of this has been tested with colour-blind readers yet. If it fails for you, please tell me.
