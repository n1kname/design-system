These run in the build, on the built tokens, and a failure stops the build. This page reads the results of that run, so the numbers can't drift from the tokens.

## What gets checked

- **Contrast, WCAG 2.2 AA:** 4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for chart marks and control edges, on the page, on cards and on sunken plates.
- **Colour blindness:** protanopia, deuteranopia and tritanopia, simulated with the Machado, Oliveira and Fernandes 2009 model, then measured in OKLab. Neighbours need 8 for protanopia and deuteranopia, and 6 for tritanopia.
- **Normal vision:** neighbours need 15 in OKLab, and any two slots need 10.
- **Greyscale:** neighbours need 1.25:1. That's my own floor, no standard sets one.
- **Labels on fills:** white or the dark ink has to reach 4.5:1 on every slot.
- **Sequential and diverging:** lightness moves in one direction in steps of at least 0.06, and the ends stand out from the surface.
