## What it's for

A short loop where movement carries the point better than a still: a flow running, a theme swapping, a filter applied. It sits on a sunken plate with a caption, like a [Figure](/components/figure/).

## When not to use it

- **A still would do.** A clip costs bandwidth and attention; use a picture when nothing important moves.
- **A walkthrough with sound or steps.** That's a video with controls, not a loop.
- **Decoration.** Motion that says nothing is noise.

## Anatomy

- **Plate:** the sunken surface, centring the clip up to `maxWidth`.
- **Clip:** muted, looping, inline, with its still frame (`poster`) showing until it plays.
- **Play/Pause:** a button in the corner, always visible.
- **Placeholder:** without `src`, a dashed box at the clip's size with its label, size and what it will show.
- **Caption:** optional.

## Variants

None. `placeholder` changes the placeholder's label.

## States

- **Arriving:** it starts on its own once it's on screen, but only when motion is welcome. Under reduced motion it waits on its still frame until someone presses Play.
- **Playing and paused:** the button says Pause or Play and its `aria-pressed` follows.
- **No video:** anyone whose browser blocks video sees the still frame.

## Tokens

`--c-surface-sunken`, `--c-surface`, `--c-border`, `--c-border-strong`, `--c-focus`, `--c-text`, `--c-text-muted`, `--font-body`, `--font-mono`, `--text-body-sm`, `--text-caption`, `--radius-lg`, `--radius-sm`, `--radius-full`, `--motion-hover`, `--ease-hover`, `--layout-measure`.

## Keyboard and screen reader

- **The button is a toggle** (`aria-pressed`) named Play or Pause, reachable by Tab, and works with Enter and Space.
- **The clip is named by `alt`,** and so is the placeholder.
- **A loop longer than five seconds can be stopped** (WCAG 2.2.2): that's what the button is for.

## Content

- **Alt:** what happens in the clip, in order, in a sentence or two.
- **Caption:** why the movement matters, not what it shows.

## Status

New. Taken from a portfolio's product clips.

## Code

```astro wrap
---
import Clip from '@n1kname/components/Clip.astro';
---

<Clip src="/video/signup.mp4" poster="/video/signup.webp" alt="The signup flow running." width={1240} height={698} />
```

A clip without `poster` fails the build: without a still it's a blank rectangle until it loads.
