Put the figures away and the routes move to fill the room. Narrow the window below 760px and the figures come under the map. The routes and figures are synthetic.

## What it's for

To keep what goes with a map on the map: the controls that change it, the legend that explains it, and the figures it adds up to. The map keeps the whole column.

## When not to use it

- **Over anything but a map** in a [map stage](/components/map-stage/). On a page, a card or a [side panel](/components/side-panel/) does it.
- **More than three panels on one map.** They cover what they explain. Move the rest under the map.
- **A long read.** A side panel scrolls, but past a screen of figures, give them a page of their own.

## Anatomy

- **Surface:** the card surface at 93%, blurred 12px behind, with a hairline border, the large radius and a soft shadow.
- **Place:** a corner, or the right edge from top to bottom.
- **Content:** a row of controls, or anything stacked with room around it.
- **Toggle:** a [FloatToggle](#floattoggle) in the controls puts a side panel away and brings it back.

## Variants

| `place` | For |
|---|---|
| `top-left` | The map's controls, such as a [Segmented](/components/segmented/) for 2D or 3D. |
| `top-right`, `bottom-right` | Something small, when the map's own buttons sit elsewhere. |
| `bottom-left` | A [legend](/components/legend/) or a [ramp legend](/components/ramp-legend/). |
| `head` | The page's title over a [map stage](/components/map-stage/) in fill mode, in its `head` slot. The stage places it, and the panels at the top start below it. In framed mode and on a phone it is a plain header above the map. |
| `side` | Figures, at the right, 300px wide (`--float-side-width`). It goes in MapStage's `aside` slot. Below 760px it stops floating and follows the map on the page. |

`layout="row"` lines controls up in a row that wraps, with tight padding. `stack`, the default, stacks content with room around it.

A corner panel leaves 48px at the far side for the map's own buttons. When a side panel floats at the right, put the map's buttons at the bottom left.

## States

- **Open and put away:** a side panel with a FloatToggle. While it's away, the toggle reads as off.
- **On a phone:** a side panel is always open, under the map, solid and without the shadow. The toggle hides, so nothing is put away out of reach.
- **Less transparency:** with the system's reduce transparency setting on, or where blur isn't drawn, the panel is solid.

## Tokens

`--c-border`, `--c-border-strong`, `--c-focus`, `--c-surface`, `--c-text`, `--c-text-secondary`, `--ease-hover`, `--motion-hover`, `--radius-lg`, `--radius-md`, `--sp-2`, `--sp-3`, `--sp-4`, `--sp-5`, `--text-body-sm`.

The surface is 93% opaque, not 90%. At 90%, muted text drops to 4.28:1 over a black map in light mode. At 93%, every text token keeps AA over any map, pure black and white included. The tokens' checks assert this for each theme (Checks, "heat and map panels").

## Keyboard and screen reader

- **A row of controls with a `label` is a group,** so a screen reader says what the buttons are for.
- **A side panel is an `aside`,** a complementary landmark, named by its `label`.
- **The toggle is a disclosure button:** `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` say what it opens and whether it's open.
- **Tab order follows the page:** the controls come before the side panel, which comes after the map.

## Content

- **Controls first, in the order they are used.** What the map shows comes before how it is drawn.
- **Name the toggle by what it shows,** such as "Figures", not "Hide".
- **Keep the side panel to what the map adds up to.** Detail about one place goes in its hover card.

## Status

New. Taken from the Map and Monthly pages of a dashboard. Changed from there: the surface is 93% instead of 90%, and it turns solid with reduce transparency.

## FloatToggle

Puts a side panel away and brings it back. It is the same height as a Segmented and hides below 760px. `for` is the side panel's id, and `open` says whether it starts open (the default).

## Code

```astro wrap
---
import MapStage from '@n1kname/components/MapStage.astro';
import FloatPanel from '@n1kname/components/FloatPanel.astro';
import FloatToggle from '@n1kname/components/FloatToggle.astro';
import Segmented from '@n1kname/components/Segmented.astro';
import Facts from '@n1kname/components/Facts.astro';
---

<MapStage id="map" label="Routes in November">
  <FloatPanel place="top-left" layout="row" label="Map controls">
    <Segmented id="view" label="View" value="2d" options={[{ value: '2d', label: '2D' }, { value: '3d', label: '3D' }]} />
    <FloatToggle for="figures">Figures</FloatToggle>
  </FloatPanel>
  <FloatPanel slot="aside" place="side" id="figures" label="The month in figures">
    <Facts id="tiles" variant="tiles" />
  </FloatPanel>
</MapStage>
```

## From script

`room()` in `mapstage.js` measures the panels that float over the map. It returns padding for MapLibre's `fitBounds` and `easeTo`, so what the map shows stays clear of them. It goes under a top panel, above a bottom one, and left of a side panel while that floats. Fit again when a side panel opens or closes, and when the map changes width.

```js
import { room } from '@n1kname/components/mapstage.js';

const box = document.getElementById('map');
const fit = () => map.fitBounds(bounds, { padding: room(box), maxZoom: 14 });
document.getElementById('figures').addEventListener('float-toggle', fit);
```
