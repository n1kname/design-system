Open **View** at the top right of the result.

## What it's for

The controls that change how a result is laid out, not what's in it: the order and the period. Behind one button that says what's set, "View: Score · 30 days", they stop competing with the filters, and the row of controls above a list stays about the list.

## When not to use it

- **Filters.** What's in the list belongs in [filter chips](/components/filter-chips/) and a [filter sheet](/components/filter-sheet/).
- **One control someone changes all the time.** Leave it in view as a [segmented](/components/segmented/) control.
- **Actions,** such as export. That's a button.

## Anatomy

- **Button:** the lead word ("View:"), what's set, and a chevron that turns over while open.
- **Panel:** the controls in the slot, each under a caption taken from its own `label`, under the button with its right edge on the button's.

## Variants

| Prop | Does |
|---|---|
| `label` | The button's lead word and the panel's name. "View". |
| `summary` | What's set, until `setViewMenu()` says. |

## States

| State | What shows |
|---|---|
| Closed | The button. |
| Hover, open | A stronger border; open, the chevron turns over. |
| Open | The panel fades in under the button over `--motion-fast`, and stays on screen at any width. |

## Tokens

`--c-border`, `--c-border-strong`, `--c-focus`, `--c-surface`, `--c-text`, `--c-text-muted`, `--c-text-secondary`, `--ease-out`, `--motion-fast`, `--radius-md`, `--sp-2` to `--sp-4`, `--text-body-sm`, `--text-caption`.

## Keyboard and screen reader

- **The panel is a native popover.** Escape or a click outside closes it, and focus goes back to the button.
- **The button says it's expanded** while the panel is open.
- **The panel is a group** named by `label`; each control keeps its own name, so the captions are hidden from screen readers rather than said twice.
- **Under reduced motion** the panel appears at once and the chevron doesn't turn.

## Content

- **The summary reads as a phrase:** "Score · 30 days", the sort first.
- **Two or three controls.** More, and it isn't a view any more.

## Status

New in 0.58.0.

## Code

```astro wrap
import ViewMenu from '@n1kname/components/ViewMenu.astro';
import Segmented from '@n1kname/components/Segmented.astro';

<ViewMenu id="view" summary="Newest · 30 days">
  <Segmented id="sort" label="Sort by" value="newest" options={sorts} />
  <Segmented id="period" label="Found in" value="30" options={periods} />
</ViewMenu>
<script>
  import { setViewMenu } from '@n1kname/components/viewmenu.js';
  document.getElementById('sort').addEventListener('segment-change', (e) => {
    sortBy(e.detail.value);
    setViewMenu(document.getElementById('view'), 'Score · 30 days');
  });
</script>
```
