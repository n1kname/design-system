## What it's for

To open a page with what matters: the conclusion, then the proof below it. A data page says how fresh its figures are here.

## When not to use it

- **Inside a card or a panel.** A panel has its own title.
- **A case page.** That has its own opening.

## Anatomy

- **Eyebrow:** optional, the section.
- **Title:** an `h1`. A finding, where the page has one.
- **Rule:** optional, the house signature: a short accent bar under the title.
- **Lede:** optional.
- **Highlight:** optional, on the words that answer the question.
- **Meta:** optional, the freshness of the data.
- **Actions:** optional, a slot. They wrap under the text when there's no room.

## Variants

- **Plain:** for a page that describes rather than answers.
- **With `rule` and `mark`:** for a page that answers a question. The rule gives the answer weight, and the highlighter points at the figure behind it.
- **`compact`:** small enough to float over a map, in a [map stage](/components/map-stage/) in fill mode. The title drops to the h3 size, the rule is shorter and closer, and the actions sit centred beside it in one row while there is room.

## States

While a page waits for data, the title, lede and meta keep their height, so nothing jumps. See [Loading and empty](/patterns/loading/).

The highlighter sweeps in once, from the left, 300ms after its words land, over 700ms. Under reduced motion it is simply there. In forced colours it becomes a thick underline.

## Tokens

`--c-accent`, `--c-bg`, `--c-text-accent`, `--c-text-secondary`, `--ease-out`, `--font-display`, `--leading-body-sm`, `--leading-h2`, `--radius-rule`, `--sp-1`, `--sp-2`, `--sp-3`, `--sp-4`, `--sp-5`, `--sp-6`, `--sp-7`, `--text-body-sm`, `--text-h2`, `--text-label`, `--tracking-h2`. Compact also uses `--leading-h3`, `--text-h3` and `--tracking-h3`.

## Keyboard and screen reader

- **One per page,** because it holds the `h1`.
- **Actions come after the title in reading order.**

## Content

- **Say the finding, not the topic,** where there is one.
- **Say freshness plainly:** "Through 31 Oct".
- **Highlight the figure, not the sentence:** two or three words, such as "6,5% lower". One highlight a page.

## Status

New. Used by a dashboard.

## Code

```astro wrap
---
import PageHeader from '@n1kname/components/PageHeader.astro';
---

<PageHeader title="The finding" lede="What this shows." meta="Through 31 Oct">
  <Button slot="actions">All figures</Button>
</PageHeader>
```

## Keep in mind

- **`ids` lets a page fill in title, lede and meta** when its data arrives at runtime.
- **`mark` marks the first place its words appear,** in the title or else the lede. A page that fills in its lede at runtime wraps the words in `<mark>` itself, and the sweep plays as they land.
