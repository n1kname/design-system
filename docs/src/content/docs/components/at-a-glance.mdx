## What it's for

The top of a case study or any long read: the whole story in a few labelled parts (a role, the problem, what was done, what changed) and the key figures. A reader who stops here leaves with the story; one who reads on knows where it's going.

## When not to use it

- **A short page.** If the page fits on a screen, it is its own summary.
- **One claim.** That's a pull-quote or a highlighted line, not four parts.
- **Data.** Use a [Stat strip](/components/stat-strip/) or a chart.

## Anatomy

- **Heading:** "At a glance" by default, small capitals in the accent text colour, as a real heading at `level` 2 or 3.
- **Parts:** each a label in the display face and a sentence (`text`), a short list (`points`) or both. Two across where there's room, stacked where there isn't.
- **Figures:** optional, under a rule: a value in the display face and what it counts.

## Variants

None. `title` changes the heading.

## States

- **Width:** parts two across from 640px of its own width, stacked below. Figures wrap.

## Tokens

`--c-surface`, `--c-border`, `--c-accent`, `--c-text`, `--c-text-secondary`, `--c-text-muted`, `--c-text-accent`, `--font-body`, `--font-display`, `--text-overline`, `--text-sub`, `--text-body`, `--text-h3`, `--text-caption`, `--radius-lg`.

## Keyboard and screen reader

- **Nothing to operate.**
- **It is a section named by its heading,** so it shows in the page's outline and a screen reader can jump to it.
- **Parts and figures are description lists,** each label read with its text.

## Content

- **Role:** what you did and for how long, honestly, in one or two sentences.
- **Problem:** the situation before, with its number if there is one.
- **What I did and what changed:** two to four points each, each a verb and its result. No point that the page doesn't back up further down.
- **Figures:** only ones the page backs up.
