## Tone

| Tone | Means |
|---|---|
| `accent` | A link you can act on, or a live state. |
| `quiet` | A descriptor, not something to click. |
| `role` | A warm tag: how something was done. |
| `focus` | The cool tag: what something was about. It uses the same teal as the second chart colour. |

## Keep in mind

- **Give it an `href` and it becomes a link.** Without one it's a plain span, so it can't look clickable by accident.
- **Put warm tags on a page or a card, not on a sunken plate.** The tint nearly matches the plate, so the fill disappears, though the text still passes.
- **It doesn't wrap.** A long label stays on one line, so keep labels short.
- **Icons only where there's a real taxonomy.** Decorative icons are worse than none.
