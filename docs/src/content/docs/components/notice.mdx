import Notice from '@n1kname/components/Notice.astro';
import Stack from '@n1kname/components/Stack.astro';

<div class="not-content">
  <Stack gap={3}>
    <Notice><strong>Figures are a day old.</strong> The sensor was offline overnight; the next reading arrives at 08:00.</Notice>
    <Notice tone="info">Averages leave out nights nobody was home.</Notice>
  </Stack>
</div>

## What it's for

Saying something about the content below it that a reader should know before trusting it, while the content still shows.

## When not to use it

- **Nothing to show.** That's an [empty state](/components/empty-state/).
- **It failed to load.** That's [Load failed](/components/load-failed/).
- **Notes on a chart's data.** Those are [Caveats](/components/caveats/), under the chart.

## Anatomy

- **Text:** one line, an optional bold lead.
- **Edge:** a coloured rule on the left for the tone.

## Variants

| Tone | For |
|---|---|
| `warning` (default) | Something is off: data is late, a source is down. |
| `info` | Something to know that isn't a problem. |

## States

It has no states of its own.

## Tokens

`--c-accent`, `--c-status-warning`, `--c-surface`, `--c-text`, `--c-text-secondary`, `--leading-body-sm`, `--radius-sm`, `--sp-3`, `--sp-4`, `--text-body-sm`.

## Keyboard and screen reader

- **Read in place,** with no role: it's there when the page loads. For news that arrives later, use a [toast](/components/toast/).

## Content

- **Lead with what's true now, then why:** "Figures are a day old. The sensor was offline overnight."
- **One line.** Two is a paragraph, and belongs on the page.

## Status

New in 0.30.0.

## Code

```astro wrap
import Notice from '@n1kname/components/Notice.astro';

```
