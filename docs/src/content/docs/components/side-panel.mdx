## What it's for

To look at detail without losing your place, such as all the figures behind a chart.

## When not to use it

- **A decision that needs an answer.** That's a small confirmation, in the page.
- **Content people come to read for itself.** Give it a page, so it has a link.
- **The main content on a phone-first page,** where it takes the whole screen anyway.

## Anatomy

- **Dialog:** native, up to 900px wide, full height.
- **Head:** the title and a Close button. It stays in view while the body scrolls.
- **Body:** a slot. Add ids to sections, so a trigger can open at one.

## Variants

None. Any element with `data-panel-open="id"` opens it. Add `data-panel-section="id"` to open at a section, so a chart's table link goes straight to its figures.

## States

| State | What happens |
|---|---|
| Opening | Slides 24px on the spring and settles, 340ms. It fades in over 260ms, and the backdrop fades with it. |
| Closing | Quicker, 180ms, and travels less: your attention has already moved on. |
| Reduced motion | Fades only. |

## Tokens

`--c-bg`, `--c-border`, `--c-text`, `--ease-out`, `--ease-spring`, `--ease-standard`, `--font-display`, `--leading-h3`, `--motion-base`, `--motion-slow`, `--motion-spring`, `--sp-3`, `--sp-4`, `--sp-5`, `--sp-6`, `--text-h3`, `--z-sticky`.

## Keyboard and screen reader

- **Focus moves in and is held there,** and returns to the trigger on close. That comes from `showModal()`.
- **Escape closes it,** and so does a click on the backdrop.
- **Scrolling past its end stays in the panel,** so the page behind it keeps your place.
- **The title names the dialog,** through `aria-labelledby`.

## Content

- **Title says what's inside:** All figures.
- **The close button says Close,** rather than a bare icon.

## Status

New. Used by a dashboard.

## From script

`openPanel(id, { title, load })` from `sidepanel.js` opens a panel for detail read on demand, such as a workout from a list. It opens at once with its title and a quiet loading shape; then what arrived settles in, or it says it failed with Try again. `load` returns a promise of HTML, escaped by the caller. The panel's state is in `data-state` (`loading`, `ready`, `failed`).

```js
import { openPanel } from '@n1kname/components/sidepanel.js';
row.addEventListener('click', () =>
  openPanel('workout', { title: 'Cycling', load: () => fetch(url).then((r) => r.json()).then(render) }),
);
```
