## What it's for

Showing the product inside a long read, such as a case study. The plate gives a light-UI capture a frame of its own, so it doesn't float on a dark page, and the caption carries the point.

## When not to use it

- **A chart or a table.** Use a [figure](/components/figure/), or a [chart figure](/components/chart-figure/) for a chart drawn at build time.
- **Several screens that belong together.** Use a [screen stack](/components/screen-stack/), or [steps](/components/steps/) for a sequence.
- **Movement.** Use a [clip](/components/clip/).

## Anatomy

- **Plate:** the sunken surface with a hairline border, centring the shot up to `maxWidth`.
- **Shot:** the picture with a hairline border and small corners. Kept as it is in dark mode, not inverted or dimmed.
- **Placeholder:** without `image`, a dashed box at the shot's size, with its label, its size and the alt text as the brief.
- **Caption:** optional, under the plate, to a reading measure.

## Variants

| Option | For |
|---|---|
| `maxWidth` | The widest it is drawn, 680px by default. The shot is served at that width and twice it. |
| `lightPlate` | Transparent artwork with dark labels: keeps the light plate in dark mode. |
| `placeholder` | The placeholder's label, "Screenshot to add" by default. |
| `width`, `height` | The shot's size in px: the placeholder's shape, and a plain URL's size so the space is kept while it loads. An import reports its own. |

## States

- **Loading:** lazy, so a shot far down the page waits until it's near.
- **No image:** the placeholder, named by its alt text for a screen reader.

## Tokens

`--c-surface-sunken`, `--c-surface`, `--c-border`, `--c-border-strong`, `--c-text`, `--c-text-muted`, `--font-body`, `--font-mono`, `--text-body-sm`, `--text-caption`, `--radius-lg`, `--radius-sm`, `--layout-measure`, `--sp-*`

## Keyboard and screen reader

- **The shot is named by `alt`,** and so is the placeholder ("Placeholder." first, so nobody mistakes it for the real thing).
- **The caption is a figcaption,** read with the figure.

## Content

- **Alt:** what the screen shows that matters, in a sentence or two. Not "screenshot of".
- **Caption:** why it matters.
- **Export big.** Drop the largest export in; the build sizes it.
