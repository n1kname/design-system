## What it's for

For a value people often want as text rather than as an action, such as an email address: the left half opens a mail client, the right half copies the address.

## When not to use it

- **A link nobody copies.** Use a linked [tag](/components/tags/).
- **A long value.** The pill doesn't wrap.

## Anatomy

- **Link half:** a linked accent tag, with an optional icon.
- **Copy half:** an icon button, joined to it by a hairline.

## Variants

None.

## States

- **Without a clipboard (or script):** the copy half isn't shown, and the link half has its round ends back.
- **Hover:** the half under the pointer deepens. Under a real pointer the whole pill lifts 2px, as one.
- **Press:** the pill gives a little and the spring brings it back.
- **Copied:** the copy icon turns into a check on the spring, for 2 seconds.

## Tokens

`--c-accent`, `--c-surface-tint`, `--c-text-accent`, `--ease-hover`, `--ease-out`, `--ease-spring`, `--motion-base`, `--motion-fast`, `--motion-hover`, `--motion-spring`, `--radius-full`

## Keyboard and screen reader

- **Two tab stops,** each with its own focus ring.
- **The copy button is named** "Copy" plus `what`, such as "Copy email address".
- **"Copied" is read out** from a live region, since a changing button label isn't announced reliably.

## Content

`what` names the value in a few words, lower case: "email address".

## Status

Used by the portfolio.
