## What it's for

How a team works, in phases: each phase a band that points on to the next, with what it is for under its title, the steps in it as rings, and a short text on what happens. Spans under the phases show what runs across them, such as research that doesn't stop when design starts.

## When not to use it

- **The idea of opening out and narrowing.** That's [Diamonds](/components/diamonds/).
- **What happened, when.** Use a [Timeline](/components/timeline/).
- **More than four phases, or more than three steps in one.** The rings get too small to read; write it as a list.

## Anatomy

- **Band:** the phase's title in the display face and what it is for under it, on its tint, on the diagrams' sunken plate. Each band points on to the next; the first is rounded at its start.
- **Steps:** hairline rings in the phase's edge colour with the step's name inside, side by side.
- **Text:** what happens in the phase, under the rings.
- **Spans:** a dashed line with a stop at each end, across the phases it runs through, its label in the middle.
- **Caption:** optional, under it all.

## Variants

None. Leave out `sub`, `steps` or `text` where a phase has none; the others still line up.

Tones run `cool`, `accent`, `quiet` by default, one per phase. Set `tone` on a phase to change it.

## States

- **Arriving:** once, as it comes into view, the phases rise into place left to right. Nothing moves under reduced motion, and without script it is simply there.
- **Narrow:** below 640px wide the phases stack, the bands become plain blocks, and each span says in words which phases it runs across ("Research: Understand to Build").
- **Forced colours:** bands and rings get a border in the system's text colour.

## Tokens

The diagrams' tones, as in [Flow diagram](/components/flow-diagram/): `--c-tag-cool-bg` and `--c-tag-cool-text` (cool), `--c-accent` and `--c-accent-edge` (accent), `--c-surface` and `--c-diagram-edge` (quiet). Also `--c-surface-sunken`, `--c-border`, `--c-diagram-line`, `--c-text`, `--c-text-secondary`, `--c-text-muted`, `--c-border-strong`, `--font-display`, `--text-body`, `--text-body-sm`, `--text-caption`, `--radius-md`, `--motion-base`, `--motion-spring`, `--ease-out`, `--ease-spring`.

## Keyboard and screen reader

- **Nothing to operate,** so nothing takes focus.
- **The phases are a list named by `title`,** each read as its title, what it is for, its steps (a list of their own) and its text.
- **Each span is read with the phases it runs across,** which the line only shows.

## Content

- **Title:** a verb and what it is about: "Define & validate".
- **Steps:** a few words each; they sit in a ring.
- **Text:** two or three sentences on who does what.

## Status

New. Used by a portfolio, for a design practice's process that had been a picture.
