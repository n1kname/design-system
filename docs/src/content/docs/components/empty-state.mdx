    </EmptyState>

## What it's for

So that nothing just looks broken. "Nothing yet", "nothing matches" and "it failed" are three different things, and each gets its own words. See [Loading and empty](/patterns/loading/).

## When not to use it

- **A value inside a table or a stat.** That's a dash.
- **While something is on its way.** Reserve the space and wait.
- **A value of zero.** Zero is a result, so show it.

## Anatomy

- **Title:** what's going on.
- **Text:** optional, what happens next.
- **Action:** optional, a slot.

## Variants

| Kind | For |
|---|---|
| `empty` | Nothing exists yet. Say when it will. |
| `filtered` | Nothing matches. That's a result, so offer a way back. |
| `error` | It failed. Say what still works, and keep the last good figures on screen. |

## States

It has no states of its own.

## Tokens

`--c-border-strong`, `--c-status-critical`, `--c-text`, `--c-text-secondary`, `--leading-caption`, `--radius-md`, `--sp-1`, `--sp-2`, `--sp-5`, `--text-body-sm`, `--text-caption`.

## Keyboard and screen reader

- **`error` has `role="alert"`,** so it's announced when it appears.
- **The other two are read in place.**

## Content

- **Say what happened and what's next,** not "No data".
- **No apology and no illustration.** It's a data page.

## Status

New. Used by a dashboard.
