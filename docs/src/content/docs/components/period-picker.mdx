## What it's for

To choose the period a page shows, such as a year. Time is mostly read a step at a time, so the arrows do the common thing, and the period itself opens every period at once.

## When not to use it

- **Periods that aren't in a line,** such as categories. They have no "next".
- **A date or a range of dates.** That needs a calendar.
- **Two or three choices.** Show them all, side by side.

## Anatomy

- **Back and forward:** a step through the periods. The line runs from the oldest to the newest and then, with `all`, to every period at once.
- **Current period:** opens the list. The period rolls as it changes: up when time goes forward, down when it goes back.
- **List:** a popover with the choice of all periods first, then the periods in a grid, newest first.

## Variants

- **With `all`:** "All years" ends the line. Without it, forward stops at the newest.
- **In the address** (`param`): the choice lives in the URL, so a link or a reload keeps it, and Back steps back through it.

## States

- **At either end:** the step that leads nowhere is disabled.
- **Changing:** the period rolls on the spring; the page's figures change with it (see Motion, "Charts changing").
- **List open:** it opens from the control on the spring and leaves quicker. The chosen period has focus and is pressed.
- **Before the periods are known:** hidden, so it never shows a period it can't step from.
- **Reduced motion:** the period changes in place.

## Keyboard and screen reader

- **Tab reaches each part.** The steps are named by `labels` ("Previous year"), and the current period reads as "Year: 2025".
- **In the list, the arrow keys move through it** in reading order, and Escape or a click outside closes it. Focus goes back to the control.
- **Each choice is a pressed or unpressed button.**

## Content

- **The period as people say it:** "2025", "Q3 2025", "All years".
- **Name the kind in `labels`,** so the steps say "Previous year", not "Previous".

## Code

```astro wrap
---
import PeriodPicker from '@n1kname/components/PeriodPicker.astro';
---

<PeriodPicker param="year" all="All years" labels={{ name: 'Year', previous: 'Previous year', next: 'Next year', list: 'Choose a year' }} />

<script>
  import { setPeriods } from '@n1kname/components/period.js';
  const picker = document.querySelector('[data-period]');
  picker.addEventListener('period-change', (e) => show(e.detail.value));
  setPeriods(picker, ['2023', '2024', '2025']); // oldest first, once they are known
</script>
```

## Keep in mind

- **Listen for `period-change`** on the element; it fires on every change, by the control or by Back.
- **Periods are oldest first,** in `periods` and in `setPeriods`.
- **Keep the figures still while the next ones load,** then let them change in place, so the page doesn't jump between periods.
