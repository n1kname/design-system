## What it's for

A little life behind a page's opening, such as a portfolio's name and lede, without an image or a video. It moves so slowly that it reads as light, not as motion.

## When not to use it

- **Behind text people read at length.** It's for an opening, not a page. Fixed, it sits behind the whole page, so keep long text on surfaces of their own, like cards.
- **More than once a page.**
- **With other motion in the same place,** such as a chart drawing itself.

## Anatomy

- **Six streaks:** blurred, on a 22° diagonal, in the accent, the chart teal and the strong border grey, each drifting at its own pace (30 to 52 seconds across).

## Variants

| Strength | For |
|---|---|
| `whisper` | Barely there. |
| `subtle` (default) | Atmosphere that never competes with the type. |
| `present` | Closer to a motion background; for a dark band, not behind body text. |

Each is stronger on the dark theme, where light needs more to show.

`fixed` fills the window instead of its section and stays put while the page scrolls past. Where the browser can tie animation to the scroll, the streaks also drift up a little slower than the text. It makes the body its own stacking context, so the streaks sit on the page colour and under everything else, and it needs no positioned parent. Use it once, near the top of the page.

## States

It has no states. Under reduced motion the streaks stand still, and fixed ones don't drift with the scroll.

## Tokens

`--c-accent`, `--c-border-strong`, `--c-chart-2`.

## Keyboard and screen reader

- **Hidden from screen readers** and from the pointer; it's decoration.

## Content

None of its own. Keep the type over it at full contrast: the streaks stay under 0.3 opacity on the light theme.

## Status

New in 0.32.0, for the portfolio's landing. `fixed` is new in 0.33.0.
