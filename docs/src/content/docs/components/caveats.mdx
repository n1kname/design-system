## What it's for

So a reader knows what the numbers can and can't carry before acting on them. It closes a data page, or a panel of figures.

## When not to use it

- **A note on one chart.** Put it in that [figure](/components/figure/)'s caption.
- **Something going wrong right now.** That's an [empty state](/components/empty-state/) of kind `error`.
- **A long method.** Link to it instead.

## Anatomy

- **Title:** a heading, at the level the page needs.
- **List:** one point per item.

## Variants

It has none. `level` sets the title's heading level, 2 by default.

## States

It has no states of its own. Filled in later, it shows the title over an empty list until the data arrives.

## Tokens

`--c-border`, `--c-text`, `--c-text-secondary`, `--font-display`, `--layout-measure`, `--leading-caption`, `--leading-sub`, `--sp-2`, `--sp-3`, `--sp-4`, `--sp-5`, `--text-caption`, `--text-sub`.

## Keyboard and screen reader

- **The title is a real heading,** so it's in the page's outline.
- **The points are a list,** so a screen reader says how many there are.

## Content

- **One limit per point,** said plainly.
- **Say what it means for the figures,** not only what's missing.

## Status

New. Used by a dashboard.
