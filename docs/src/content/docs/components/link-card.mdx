## What it's for

To send someone to a page that is worth a whole card of explanation: a case on a portfolio index, a report in a list. The whole card is the target, so there's one link, not three.

## When not to use it

- **A card with several actions.** Use a plain surface with real links and buttons inside.
- **A list you scan.** A row of text links reads faster than a stack of cards.
- **Data on a dashboard.** A card that moves under the pointer suggests it opens something; a figure that doesn't shouldn't move.

## Anatomy

- **Media:** optional, in the `media` slot. Left of the text from 640px of card, above it below that. Its frame clips, so the picture can lean in without growing the card.
- **Body:** the default slot. An overline, a heading, a line or two, tags.
- **Surface:** the card fill and edge.

## Variants

With or without media. `transition` names the picture for a cross-document view transition, so it grows into the picture with the same name on the page it opens. Give that one `view-transition-class: media`; `page-change.css` animates the move on the spring.

## States

- **Hover:** the edge and the fill deepen. Under a real pointer the card also lifts 2px and the picture leans in 2%, on the spring.
- **Press:** the card gives a little and the spring brings it back.
- **Focus:** the ring goes round the whole card.
- **Reduced motion:** nothing moves; only the edge and the fill answer.

## Tokens

`--c-accent-edge`, `--c-border`, `--c-surface`, `--c-surface-tint`, `--ease-hover`, `--ease-out`, `--ease-spring`, `--motion-base`, `--motion-hover`, `--motion-spring`, `--radius-lg`, `--radius-sm`

## Keyboard and screen reader

- **It's one link,** so it's one tab stop and is read as a link named by everything inside it. Keep that short: a heading and a line, not a page.
- **Don't put another link or a button inside.** A link inside a link is invalid and breaks both.
- **The picture needs alt text** only if it says something the heading doesn't. Otherwise leave it empty.

## Content

- **Lead with the outcome,** in the heading. The project name goes in the overline.
- **One picture per card,** cropped from the page it opens, so the two can't drift apart.

## Status

Used by the portfolio's case index.
