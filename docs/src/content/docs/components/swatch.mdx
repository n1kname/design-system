## What it's for

To tie a word to its colour in a chart: a series in a table cell, a category on a chip, a kind in a list. The same shapes a [Legend](/components/legend/) draws.

## When not to use it

- **On its own.** A swatch never tells things apart by itself; a word goes beside it.
- **Good or bad.** Status takes its icon and label.

## Anatomy

A mark, `aria-hidden`: the word beside it says what it is.

## Variants

- **dot:** a category or a kind (default).
- **square:** a bar or an area.
- **line:** a line.
- **ring:** a second kind of dot.

## States

None.

## Tokens

Any chart token, such as `--c-chart-3` or `--c-chart-neutral`.

## Keyboard and screen reader

Hidden from screen readers; the word beside it is read.

## Content

Beside a word, always.

## Status

New. Six hand-made dots in a dashboard became this.

## Code

```astro
---
import Swatch from '@n1kname/components/Swatch.astro';
```
