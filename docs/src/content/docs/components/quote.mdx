Quotes are evidence, so the source is required. A quote with no source is decoration.

<div class="not-content">
  <Quote from="A fictional driver, usability session">

  <p>I didn't know it had stopped until I got back to the car.</p>

  </Quote>
</div>

## Keep in mind

- **The text goes in a paragraph.** In MDX, leave a blank line around it so it parses as one.
- **`from` says who and where.** A role and a setting is enough when you can't name the person.
