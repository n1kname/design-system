## What it's for

To show what someone said, as evidence. So the source is required.

## When not to use it

- **Decoration.** A quote with no source is decoration.
- **Praise for the work.** This is for what you learned.

## Anatomy

- **Text:** a paragraph.
- **Source:** `from`, who said it and in what setting.

## Variants

None.

## States

It has no states of its own.

## Tokens

`--c-border`, `--c-surface`, `--c-surface-tint`, `--c-text-accent`, `--c-text-muted`, `--font-display`, `--leading-body-sm`, `--radius-full`, `--radius-lg`, `--sp-4`, `--sp-5`, `--sp-6`, `--sp-7`, `--text-body-sm`, `--text-h3`.

## Keyboard and screen reader

- **It's a `figure` with a `blockquote` and a `figcaption`,** so the source stays tied to the words.

## Content

- **`from` says who and where.** A role and a setting is enough when you can't name the person.
- **Keep their words,** and cut rather than rewrite.

## Status

Used by the portfolio.

## Code

```astro wrap
---
import Quote from '@n1kname/components/Quote.astro';
---

<Quote from="Who said it, and in what setting">
  <p>What they said.</p>
</Quote>
```

## Keep in mind

- **In MDX, leave a blank line around the text** so it parses as a paragraph.
