## What it's for

Two to four things compared on the same few points, where each thing deserves its own card: sources of evidence and what each could and couldn't see, a problem and the rule it became, one thing split into parts and where each part went. It says the same as a small table, but you can see the difference before you read it: a check for what something gives, a dashed box for what it misses, an arrow from before to after.

## When not to use it

- **Many rows, or numbers to sort and scan.** Use a [Data table](/components/data-table/).
- **How the things connect.** That's a [Flow diagram](/components/flow-diagram/).
- **Outcomes per area, with a status.** Use an [Outcome table](/components/outcome-table/).

## Anatomy

- **Card:** on the surface with a hairline border, an icon disc in the card's tone (or its number, without an icon), a name, and an optional kicker under it.
- **Notes:** a small label and a sentence or two. A note's `mark` sets how it's drawn.
- **Caption:** optional, under the cards.

## Variants

| Mark | Drawn as | For |
|---|---|---|
| `gain` | A tinted box with a check | What something gives |
| `gap` | A dashed box with a crossed-out eye, the text a step quieter | What it misses |
| `before` | A box tinted with the critical colour | What it was |
| `after` | A box in the card's tone; after a `before`, an arrow points into it | What it became |
| `plain` | Label and text, no box | Anything else, such as where it applies |

Cards alternate `accent` and `cool` unless a card sets `tone` (`accent`, `cool` or `quiet`). `columns` sets how many sit in a row where there's room, from 1 to 4; 2 by default.

## States

- **Arriving:** once, as it comes into view, the cards rise in, 90ms apart. Nothing moves under reduced motion, and without script it is simply there.
- **Narrow:** below 560px wide, the cards stack in one column.
- **Forced colours:** cards, discs and note boxes are drawn in the system's text colour.

## Tokens

`--c-surface`, `--c-border`, `--c-border-strong` and `--c-diagram-line` for cards, dashed boxes and the arrow. Tones as in [Flow diagram](/components/flow-diagram/): `--c-surface-tint`, `--c-accent` and `--c-accent-edge` (accent), `--c-tag-cool-bg` and `--c-tag-cool-text` (cool), `--c-diagram-edge` and `--c-text-secondary` (quiet). `--c-status-critical` for a before. Also `--c-text`, `--c-text-muted`, `--font-display`, `--text-sub`, `--text-body-sm`, `--text-caption`, `--radius-md`, `--radius-lg`, `--motion-reveal`, `--ease-out`.

## Keyboard and screen reader

- **Nothing to operate,** so nothing takes focus.
- **It is a list named by `title`,** each card a heading and a description list: the note's label, then its text. The marks' icons and the arrow are hidden; the labels say what each note is.

## Content

- **Names:** a word or three: "Usage data", "Filter chips".
- **Notes:** one short sentence, ending in a full stop. The same labels on every card, so they compare.
- **Gaps are honest:** a source that can see everything doesn't need this component.

## Status

New. Used by a portfolio, for comparisons that had been plain tables.
