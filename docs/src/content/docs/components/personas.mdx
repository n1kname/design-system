## What it's for

The roles a service has to work for, compared side by side: who each one is, what they need to do, and a job story or a real quote in their voice. For the roles on a story map or a blueprint, where seeing them next to each other is the point.

## When not to use it

- **One persona in depth,** with goals, frustrations and a photo. That's a page of its own, not a row.
- **Comparing things, not people,** such as sources of evidence. Use [Compare cards](/components/compare-cards/).
- **How the roles work together.** That's a [Flow diagram](/components/flow-diagram/).

## Anatomy

- **Avatar:** an icon disc in the person's tone. Icons, not faces: a role is not a stock photo.
- **Name and role:** the name in the display face, and who they are in a few words under it.
- **Notes:** what they need to do (labelled by `needsLabel`), and any more labelled lines, such as "Later".
- **Voice:** a job story or a quote, in italics on a soft tint of their tone (10%, as the [Notice](/components/notice/)), at the foot of the column so the voices line up. Not a rule down one side: that reads as a coloured bar on a box. The label over it takes the tone's readable colour.
- **Columns:** open, with a hairline between them and nothing around them, so a page with boxes doesn't get more.

## Variants

One layout. Tones alternate `accent` and `cool` unless a person sets `tone` (`accent`, `cool` or `quiet`). A person can leave out the role, the notes or the quote.

## States

- **Arriving:** once, as it comes into view, the people rise in, 90ms apart. Nothing moves under reduced motion, and without script they are simply there.
- **Narrow:** up to four columns where there's room; below 640px wide two, below 420px one, with a rule between people instead of beside them.
- **Forced colours:** the avatar's edge and a border round the quote are drawn in the system's text colour, since the tint drops out.

## Tokens

The diagrams' tones, as in [Flow diagram](/components/flow-diagram/): `--c-surface-tint`, `--c-accent` and `--c-accent-edge` (accent), `--c-tag-cool-bg` and `--c-tag-cool-text` (cool), `--c-surface`, `--c-diagram-edge` and `--c-text-secondary` (quiet). Also `--c-border`, `--c-text`, `--c-text-muted`, `--radius-md`, `--font-display`, `--text-sub`, `--text-body-sm`, `--text-caption`, `--motion-reveal`, `--ease-out`.

## Keyboard and screen reader

- **Nothing to operate,** so nothing takes focus.
- **It is a list named by `title`,** each person a heading, then their notes as a description list, then their quote as a blockquote. The avatar is hidden.

## Content

- **Names:** the role, not a made-up first name: "Fleet manager", not "Frank".
- **Role line:** who they are in a few words.
- **Needs:** one sentence, a verb first.
- **Voice:** a job story ("When…, I want…, so…") or a real quote, never an invented one. Say in the caption where it comes from.

## Status

New. Used by a portfolio, for the four roles on a story map that had been plain cards. Since 0.59.0.
