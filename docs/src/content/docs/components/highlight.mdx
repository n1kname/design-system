## What it's for

To point at the words a heading or a lede is about, such as the figure that answers the question. It sits low, like a pen, and wraps line by line.

## When not to use it

- **Emphasis in running text.** Use bold.
- **More than two or three marks on a page.** Then nothing stands out.
- **A link.** It would look like a visited state.

## Anatomy

- **The band:** the accent mixed into the page colour, so it holds up in dark.
- **The words:** keep their own colour.

## Variants

`n` gives a mark its place among the marks on the page, so several sweep one after the other. [Page header](/components/page-header/) uses the same highlighter for its `mark` prop.

## States

- **Arriving:** sweeps in 300ms after load, then 450ms per mark. A page can set `--highlight-delay` and `--highlight-step` to wait for its own opening.
- **Reduced motion:** the band is simply there.
- **Forced colours:** an underline instead of the band.

## Tokens

`--c-accent`, `--c-bg`, `--ease-out`

## Keyboard and screen reader

It's a `mark` element, which some screen readers announce as highlighted. Mark the words that matter, not decoration.

## Content

A few words, never a whole sentence.

## Status

Used by the portfolio and, through Page header, a dashboard.
