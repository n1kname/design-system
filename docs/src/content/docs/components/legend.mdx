## What it's for

To say what each colour or mark in a chart means, in words, right under the chart.

## When not to use it

- **One series.** The chart's title already says what it is.
- **A few lines that can be labelled at their ends.** A direct label is read faster than a key.
- **A colour scale.** A scale with steps needs its values, not names.

## Anatomy

- **Keys:** a mark, then its label, in a row that wraps.
- **Mark:** drawn the way the chart draws it, in the same colour.

## Variants

| Mark | For |
|---|---|
| `fill` | Bars, areas and cells. The default. |
| `line` | A line chart. |
| `dot` | A dot plot. |
| `hollow` | A second kind of dot. |
| `stripes` | Something known and set aside. |
| `empty` | An outline only, for "nothing here". |
| `faint` | A paler swatch, for an estimate, such as a [bar](/components/bars/) drawn with `fade`. |
| `dashed` | Paler still with a dashed edge, for a projection of something still running. |

## States

It has no states.

## Tokens

`--c-border-strong`, `--c-text-secondary`, `--leading-caption`, `--sp-2`, `--sp-3`, `--sp-4`, `--text-caption`.

## Keyboard and screen reader

- **It's a list with a label,** "Legend" unless you name it, so a screen reader says how many keys there are.
- **The marks are decoration;** the words carry the meaning.
- **Hide it with `aria-hidden`** when the chart's text alternative already says what each colour is.

## Content

- **Name what the reader sees,** such as "This year", not a column name.
- **Keep the order of the chart:** left to right, or bottom to top.

## Status

New. Used by a dashboard.
