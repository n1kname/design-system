## What it's for

Anything a person types or picks in a form: a filter, a note, a setting. The label is always visible above the field, so it doesn't vanish once someone starts typing.

## When not to use it

- **A search box.** That's [Search field](/components/search-field/), with its clear button.
- **Two to five fixed options.** Show them all with [Segmented](/components/segmented/) or [Filter chips](/components/filter-chips/).
- **Signing in.** [Sign in](/components/sign-in/) has its own fields.

## Anatomy

- **Label:** above, always shown.
- **Field:** an input, a select or a textarea, 44px tall.
- **Hint:** optional, under it: what to put in.
- **Error:** optional, under it in the critical colour: what's wrong and how to fix it.

## Variants

| Prop | Does |
|---|---|
| `as` | `input` (default), `select` or `textarea`. |
| `type` | For an input: `text`, `email`, `url`, `number`, `date`… |
| `options` | For a select. |
| `hint`, `error` | The lines under it. An error marks the field invalid. |

## States

| State | What shows |
|---|---|
| Hover | A stronger border. |
| Focus | The focus ring. |
| Invalid | A critical border and the error line. |

## Tokens

`--c-border-strong`, `--c-focus`, `--c-status-critical`, `--c-surface`, `--c-text`, `--c-text-muted`, `--c-text-secondary`, `--leading-caption`, `--radius-md`, `--sp-1`, `--sp-3`, `--text-body-sm`, `--text-caption`, `--text-label`.

## Keyboard and screen reader

- **The label is a real `<label>`,** so clicking it focuses the field.
- **The hint and the error are tied to the field** with `aria-describedby`, and an error sets `aria-invalid`.
- **16px text at least,** so iOS doesn't zoom in on focus.

## Content

- **Labels are nouns, in sentence case:** "Alert threshold", not "Enter your alert threshold".
- **An error says how to fix it,** not just that something is wrong.
