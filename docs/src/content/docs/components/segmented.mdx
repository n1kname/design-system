## What it's for

To choose one of two to five fixed options that change how something is shown: how many rows, how words match, a view.

## When not to use it

- **Values with counts or colours,** such as categories. Use [Filter chips](/components/filter-chips/).
- **Periods in a line.** Use the [Period picker](/components/period-picker/).
- **Navigation between pages.** Use [Page nav](/components/page-nav/).

## Anatomy

- **Track,** sunken.
- **Marker,** a raised surface under the chosen option.
- **Options,** buttons.

## Variants

None. Keep the labels short.

## States

- **Chosen:** on the marker, in bold.
- **Changing:** the marker slides on the spring, and widens or narrows to the option.
- **Reduced motion:** the marker moves at once.

## Tokens

`--c-bg`, `--c-border`, `--c-border-strong`, `--c-focus`, `--c-surface`, `--c-surface-sunken`, `--c-text`, `--c-text-secondary`, `--ease-hover`, `--ease-out`, `--ease-spring`, `--motion-base`, `--motion-hover`, `--motion-spring`, `--radius-md`, `--sp-3`, `--text-body-sm`.

## Keyboard and screen reader

- **A group named by `label`** of pressed and unpressed buttons. Tab reaches each; Enter or Space chooses.

## Content

- **One or two words per option,** in the same form: "Top 20", "Top 50".

## Status

New. Taken from a dashboard's Places page and its labs.

## Code

```astro wrap
---
import Segmented from '@n1kname/components/Segmented.astro';
---

<Segmented id="top" label="How many" value="50" options={[{ value: '20', label: 'Top 20' }, { value: '50', label: 'Top 50' }]} />

<script>
  document.getElementById('top').addEventListener('segment-change', (e) => show(Number(e.detail.value)));
</script>
```
