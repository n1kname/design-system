## What it's for

The cases on a portfolio index, when the work is worth looking at before it's worth reading: each tile shows the product in its own colour, says what it achieved, and opens the case. One wide tile leads; the rest sit two to a row.

## When not to use it

- **A list someone scans or searches.** A text list reads faster than a wall of colour.
- **Cases without pictures worth showing.** Use a [link card](/components/link-card/), or give the tile one honest photo with `image`.
- **More than a handful.** Six tiles is a page; twenty is a scroll. Pick.

## Anatomy

- **Panel:** the `tone` as a soft diagonal gradient with a faint dot grid, 24px corners. White text, so the tone must be deep.
- **Words:** an overline (organisation, years, state), the title in the display face, one line, and an optional note such as "Concepts".
- **Figures:** up to four, two by two under a hairline, on a wide tile only.
- **Pictures:** two or three screens (laptop or phone) or one picture, running off the bottom and right edge. The first screen sits in front.

## Variants

| Option | For |
|---|---|
| `layout="wide"` | The leading case: words and figures left, screens right. Spans the row. |
| `layout="half"` | Two to a row: words on top, screens underneath. Any tile under 760px wide uses this layout. |
| `device="phone"` | Tall phone screens in a thin bezel. |
| `image` | One picture, such as a photo, for a case without screens. |
| `layers` | Separate pictures on a stage, each placed in percent and given a `depth`: on hover the deeper ones float up further, so a card lifts off the page it came from. Give a layer `float: true` and it bobs 16px down and back on its own, every 6 seconds, whatever the others do. `stage` sets the stage's ratio. |
| `ink="dark"` | Dark words and pattern for a light tone, such as a yellow. |
| `pattern` | The texture: `dots` (default), `grid`, `lines`, `plus`, `waves` or `none`. `patternFade={false}` keeps it even across the tile. |
| `note` | A label under the words, such as "Concepts" or "Shipped". |
| `transition` | A view-transition name on the pictures, so they grow into the cover on the page the tile opens. Only when the two share a shape: a wide tile's art morphing into a 20:9 cover looks warped, so let the cover play its own entrance instead. |

## States

- **Arrival:** tiles below the fold arm on load; as each scrolls into view its screens rise 48px and fade in, front first, 90ms apart. Tiles already in view are simply there.
- **Float:** a layer with `float` bobs slowly on its own, down and back, adding to the hover lift.
- **Hover:** the tile lifts 3px with a soft shadow, and the screens fan out a little (phones also tip).
- **Focus:** the same as hover, plus a ring round the tile.
- **Reduced motion:** nothing moves.

## Tokens

`--font-display`, `--text-caption`, `--text-body-sm`, `--radius-full`, `--motion-spring`, `--ease-spring`, `--ease-soft`, `--motion-hover`, `--c-focus`, `--sp-*`

## Keyboard and screen reader

- **It's one link,** named title first, then the line, the overline and the note, so the case comes before its dates. Screens get empty alt by default, so the name stays short.
- **Don't put another link inside.**
- **The figures are a description list,** read as value and label.

## Content

- **Title: the product's name. Line: what it achieved.** Outcome first, in one sentence.
- **Figures that a stranger understands**, with a label that says what is counted.
- **Label concepts.** A tile looks finished; `note` says when it isn't.

## Keep in mind

- **Layers load at the size they show.** Each layer asks for a file sized to its share of the stage, so a small card cut out of a page stays a small download.

- **Let the grid stretch the tiles,** so two half tiles in a row are the same height; the pictures fill whatever is left under the words.
