## What it's for

A career framework a reader should be able to explore: the levels as a ladder, forking where one level has two routes, and for the level picked, what it is in one line, what it's responsible for, what changes from the level before (what a promotion to it rests on), and a line on each skill area. On request, a table puts every level side by side.

## When not to use it

- **A process.** Use [Phases](/components/phases/) or [Diamonds](/components/diamonds/).
- **A team's structure.** That's a [Flow diagram](/components/flow-diagram/).
- **A ladder with no descriptions.** A row of names says nothing a sentence wouldn't.

## Anatomy

- **Ladder:** one node per level, joined by lines; levels with the same `rank` sit one above the other, and the lines fork to them and join after. The fork's nodes are tinted. The level picked is the one solid node.
- **Panel:** the level's title, its label, route and experience in one line, and the level in one sentence. Under that, side by side where there's room: what it's responsible for and what changes from the level before (its `changes`), then its skills.
- **Compare:** only with `compare`, a disclosure with every level as a column and every skill area as a row. It scrolls sideways on a narrow screen.
- **Caption:** optional.

## Variants

None. Leave out `areas` for no skills, `changes` on a level where nothing comes before it, and `compare` for no table. `scopeLabel`, `changesLabel` and `skillsLabel` rename the panel's headings.

## States

- **Picked:** solid, `aria-selected="true"`, its panel shown. Picking changes the panel at once.
- **Hover and focus:** hover darkens a node's edge; focus gets the focus ring.
- **Narrow:** below 640px wide the ladder is a list, and the panel's parts stack.
- **Without script:** the level given as `selected` shows, and the table, when there is one, holds every level.

## Keyboard and screen reader

- **The ladder is a tab list named by `title`.** Tab reaches the level picked; the arrow keys move along the ladder in reading order and pick as they go, Home and End go to the ends.
- **Each panel is a tab panel** labelled by its level, and takes focus, so Tab from the ladder goes into it.
- **The table, when there is one, has row and column headers**, and its scroll area is focusable and named, so it can be scrolled from the keyboard.

## Content

- **Titles:** the job titles as written in the framework.
- **One line:** what sets the level apart, in one sentence.
- **What changes:** two to four steps, each what the level adds, starting with a verb.
- **Skills:** one line per area, starting with a verb.
