## What it's for

One value read against its own recent past, such as today's price against the last 90 days, with a level to read it against, such as the price someone is waiting for. It answers "is this low for it?" at a glance.

## When not to use it

- **Many rows of figures, each with its range.** That's [range bars](/components/range-bars/).
- **How a value moved over time.** That's [lines](/components/lines/); this one keeps only the low, the high and now.
- **A value with no range yet,** such as a first reading. Say the value.

## Anatomy

- **Track:** the scale, from the lowest to the highest of the low, the high, the value and the reference, so each of them shows.
- **Band:** from the period's low to its high, in a quiet tint of the second chart colour.
- **Dot:** the value, ringed in the surface so it reads over the band.
- **Tick:** the reference, dashed as a reference line in a chart.
- **Ends:** the low and the high under the band's ends.
- **Key:** the dot and the tick in words, with their values: "Today €2.449", "Target €2.420".

## Variants

| Prop | Does |
|---|---|
| `low`, `high`, `value` | The band and the dot. Leave `low` out to hold the room until `setRangePosition()` fills it. |
| `reference` | A dashed tick, such as a target. Optional. |
| `format` | Words for a number, such as a price. |
| `valueLabel`, `referenceLabel`, `rangeLabel` | Names in the key and for a screen reader: "Today", "Target", "Range" by default. |
| `tone` | `good`: the dot in the status green, for a value in the person's favour, such as at or under its target. The key still says it. |

## States

| State | What shows |
|---|---|
| Waiting | Its room, empty, so nothing moves when it's filled. |
| Filled | The scale, the ends and the key. |
| Value or reference outside the band | The scale widens to take it in; the band stays the period's own. |
| Low equals high | A short band, still drawn. |

## Keyboard and screen reader

- **One image with a name** that says it all in a sentence: "Today €2.449. Range €2.399 to €2.649. Target €2.420."
- **Nothing to focus:** it has no controls.
- **Colour never carries it alone:** the key names the dot and the tick, and a good value is also said by the page, in a chip or a line.

## Content

- **Say the period near it,** such as in its heading: "Where today's price sits, last 90 days".
- **Labels of one word.** The key is a line, not a sentence.

## Code

```astro wrap
import RangePosition from '@n1kname/components/RangePosition.astro';

<RangePosition id="range" />

  import { setRangePosition } from '@n1kname/components/rangeposition.js';
  setRangePosition(document.getElementById('range'), {
    low: 2399, high: 2649, value: 2449, reference: 2420,
    format: (v) => `€${v.toLocaleString('nl-NL')}`,
    tone: 2449 <= 2420 ? 'good' : undefined,
  });
</script>
```
