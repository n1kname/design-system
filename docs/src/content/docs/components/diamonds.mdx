## What it's for

A way of working drawn as diamonds: each phase opens out to look wide and narrows to a decision, with the moments between phases, such as a kick-off or a launch, as dots on the line. A last phase can repeat instead, as smaller diamonds that fade out, for the work that keeps going after launch.

## When not to use it

- **Steps with their own names and owners.** Use [Phases](/components/phases/): bands with steps under them say who does what, which diamonds can't.
- **Who works with whom.** That's a [Flow diagram](/components/flow-diagram/).
- **A plan with dates.** Use a [Timeline](/components/timeline/): a diamond has no time axis.

## Anatomy

- **Moments:** a small stop level with the diamonds' points, its name under it, on a short line from the point before to the point after with an arrowhead into the next phase. The line never crosses a diamond.
- **Phases:** a diamond with soft corners in two halves, on the diagrams' sunken plate: a tint with a hairline edge, the opening half a little stronger. Each half's label is centred in the middle of that half, at one size, and wraps when it's too long for it. The phase's name and motto sit under the diamond, and under those, each half's note: a short title and the question it asks, side by side where there's room, one under the other where there isn't.
- **A repeating phase:** three smaller diamonds with a dashed edge that fade out, then its name and one note.
- **Caption:** optional, under it all.

## Variants

| Phase | For |
|---|---|
| `halves` | A phase that opens out and narrows: two labels, and a note on each |
| `repeat` | Work that goes on after launch, with no end to draw |

Tones run `cool`, `accent`, `quiet` by default, one per phase; a repeating phase is `quiet`. Set `tone` on a phase to change it.

## States

- **Arriving:** once, as it comes into view, the parts rise into place left to right, 80ms apart. Nothing moves under reduced motion, and without script it is simply there.
- **Narrow:** below 560px wide the phases stack, each moment between them as a stop and its name.
- **Forced colours:** the edges are drawn in the system's text colour on the background.

## Tokens

The diagrams' tones, as in [Flow diagram](/components/flow-diagram/): `--c-tag-cool-bg` and `--c-tag-cool-text` (cool), `--c-surface-tint`, `--c-accent` and `--c-accent-edge` (accent), `--c-surface` and `--c-diagram-edge` (quiet), with `--c-text` on them. Also `--c-surface-sunken`, `--c-border`, `--c-text-secondary`, `--c-text-muted`, `--font-display`, `--font-body`, `--text-sub`, `--text-body-sm`, `--text-caption`, `--motion-base`, `--motion-spring`, `--ease-out`, `--ease-spring`.

## Keyboard and screen reader

- **Nothing to operate,** so nothing takes focus.
- **It is a list named by `title`,** read in order: a moment, then a phase's notes, its two labels and its name.
- **The shapes are hidden** from screen readers; every word is text.

## Content

- **Labels:** one or two words, a verb where you can: Discover, Define.
- **Notes:** the question each half asks, in a few words.
- **Name and motto:** what the phase is called, and what it is for in one short line.

## Status

New. Used by a portfolio, for a design practice's way of working that had been a picture.
