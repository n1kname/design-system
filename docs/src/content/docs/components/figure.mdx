## What it's for

To give every chart and table the same frame. [Ranked bars](/components/ranked-bars/) and the [donut](/components/donut/) sit in one already.

## When not to use it

- **A picture.** Use a plain `figure`.
- **A number on its own.** Use [Facts](/components/facts/) tiles.

## Anatomy

- **Title and sub:** optional. Sub says what it shows and the unit. With `level`, the title is a heading at that level.
- **Actions:** optional, a slot, such as a link to the table.
- **Body:** the chart. The body is a container, so what's inside can respond to the room it has.
- **Caption:** optional, below the field.
- **In a [Grid](/components/layout/):** the cards in a row are one height, and their heads share a height, so a sub on two lines in one card doesn't push its chart below the others.

## Variants

| Variant | For |
|---|---|
| `plate` | A sunken field, for long reads. The default. |
| `card` | A surface, for dashboards. |

## States

It holds no state. The chart inside reserves its own height while it waits.

## Tokens

`--c-border`, `--c-surface`, `--c-surface-sunken`, `--c-text`, `--c-text-muted`, `--font-display`, `--layout-measure`, `--leading-caption`, `--leading-sub`, `--radius-lg`, `--sp-1`, `--sp-3`, `--sp-4`, `--sp-5`, `--sp-6`, `--text-body-sm`, `--text-caption`, `--text-sub`.

## Keyboard and screen reader

- **It's a `figure` with a `figcaption`.**
- **Give it a `level` when it is a section of the page,** so its title is in the page's outline. Without one the title is plain text.
- **A chart needs its own `alt`, and a table beside it.** Every value has to be reachable without a mouse.

## Content

- **Titles say what's plotted.**
- **Captions say what it means,** or how to read it.

## Status

Used by the portfolio. Also used by a dashboard.
