## What it's for

A chart in a long read, drawn once per width at build time rather than scaled. A scaled SVG has tiny text on a phone and huge text on a wide screen; three drawings keep the text one size and let the chart use the room it has.

## When not to use it

- **A chart that changes on the page.** Draw it in the browser.
- **A picture.** Use a [screenshot](/components/screenshot/).
- **A dashboard tile with actions.** Use a [figure](/components/figure/) around the chart.

## Anatomy

- **Panel:** the surface with a hairline border and large corners. It is its own container, so it picks a drawing by its own width, not the window's.
- **Title:** optional, small and semibold at the top.
- **Drawings:** `narrow` (320), `wide` (about 720) and an optional `full` (960). Never scaled up, centred when the panel is wider.
- **Legend:** the `legend` slot, under the chart, inside the panel.
- **Caption:** optional, under the panel.

## Variants

| Option | For |
|---|---|
| `full` drawing | A chart worth the width of a wide screen, from a 960px panel up. |
| `narrowBelow={420}` | A half-width panel, whose wide drawing still fits down to 420px. 560 by default. |
| `as="div"` | One panel of a larger figure, such as two charts side by side under one legend and caption: no margin and no caption of its own. |

## States

- **Arrival:** the chart plays in once when it scrolls into view, through the charts' `enter`. Under reduced motion it is simply there.

## Tokens

`--c-surface`, `--c-border`, `--c-text`, `--c-text-muted`, `--text-body-sm`, `--radius-lg`, `--layout-measure`, `--sp-*`

## Keyboard and screen reader

- **One image, named by `alt`.** Only one drawing shows at a time, and the alt speaks for all of them.
- **Write the alt as the finding:** what goes up, what goes down, and the figures that matter.

## Content

- **The caption counts:** what is measured, over what period, and what's still running.
- **Draw the narrow one for a phone,** with fewer ticks and shorter words, not the wide one squeezed.

## Status

New. Taken from a portfolio's case charts.

## Code

```astro wrap
---
import ChartFigure from '@n1kname/components/ChartFigure.astro';
import { bars, renderPlot, NARROW, WIDE } from '@n1kname/charts';

const spec = (width) => bars({ rows, x: 'month', series }, width);
const chart = {
  narrow: renderPlot({ width: NARROW, ...spec(NARROW) }),
  wide: renderPlot({ width: WIDE, ...spec(WIDE) }),
  full: renderPlot({ width: 960, ...spec(960) }),
};
---

<ChartFigure chart={chart} alt="What the chart shows, as a finding." caption="What it counts." />
```
