## What it's for

How something got made, stage by stage: each with its number, its name, a line on what it was for, and a picture of it. The words are text and only the screens are pictures, so it reads at any size and reflows on a phone.

## When not to use it

- **A process with phases and steps inside them.** That's [Phases](/components/phases/).
- **What happened when.** Use a [Timeline](/components/timeline/).
- **One picture with a caption.** That's a [Figure](/components/figure/).

## Anatomy

- **Number:** two digits in the mono face, in the accent text colour. The numbers carry the order, so there are no arrows to break when it reflows.
- **Name:** the stage, and a line on what it was for under it.
- **Picture:** on a small sunken plate, at its own height. An imported image goes through `astro:assets` (sized and converted to webp); an address is used as it is.
- **Placeholder:** without a picture, a dashed slot with the stage's name.
- **Caption:** optional.

## Variants

None.

## States

- **Width:** four across, two across below 860px of its own width, one below 480px. It follows the space it has, not the window.

## Tokens

`--c-surface-sunken`, `--c-surface`, `--c-border`, `--c-border-strong`, `--c-text`, `--c-text-muted`, `--c-text-accent`, `--font-body`, `--font-mono`, `--text-sub`, `--text-body-sm`, `--text-caption`, `--radius-sm`, `--layout-measure`.

## Keyboard and screen reader

- **Nothing to operate.**
- **It is an ordered list,** so the order is read out as well as numbered.
- **Each picture has its `alt`;** a placeholder is named as one.

## Content

- **Names:** short, a noun: "Wireframes", "Shipped product".
- **Body:** who it was for, or what it settled, in one sentence.

## Status

New. Taken from a portfolio's case studies, where it replaced a picture with the headings baked in.
