## What it's for

One question answered several ways, step for step: three countries and how each sets a price and what the design does about it, three channels and how each one works. Each track is a row and each row reads as a small flow, so the differences show in the same place on every row. Drawn in the same family as [Phases](/components/phases/), for a page that has had enough boxes.

## When not to use it

- **One flow, not several.** Use [Phases](/components/phases/) or [Steps](/components/steps/).
- **Things compared on what they give and miss,** not on steps. Use [Compare cards](/components/compare-cards/).
- **Many tracks, or numbers.** More than five rows reads as a table; use a [Data table](/components/data-table/).
- **How the tracks connect to each other.** That's a [Flow diagram](/components/flow-diagram/).

## Anatomy

- **Plate:** sunken, with a hairline, as in Phases.
- **Step names:** once, over their columns.
- **Track name:** on the left, with a disc holding an icon or a short mark such as "NL", and an optional line under it.
- **Bands:** one per step, each pointing on to the next, a sentence in each. The last step is tinted in the accent, the ones before it quiet, so the eye lands on the answer.
- **Caption:** optional, under the plate.

## Variants

One layout. Tracks alternate `accent` and `cool` discs unless a track sets `tone`. `stepTones` sets the tone of each column of bands (`accent`, `cool` or `quiet`). A disc takes `icon` or `short`; with neither, the track has no disc.

## States

- **Arriving:** once, as it comes into view, the tracks slide in, 110ms apart. Nothing moves under reduced motion, and without script they are simply there.
- **Narrow:** below 640px wide each track stacks: its name, then its steps one under the other, each band named and an arrow down between them.
- **Forced colours:** the bands lose their points and get a border in the system's text colour, and so do the discs.

## Tokens

`--c-surface-sunken` and `--c-border` for the plate; `--c-diagram-line` for the arrows. Tones as in [Flow diagram](/components/flow-diagram/): `--c-accent` and `--c-accent-edge` (accent), `--c-tag-cool-bg` and `--c-tag-cool-text` (cool), `--c-surface`, `--c-diagram-edge` and `--c-text-secondary` (quiet). Also `--c-text`, `--c-text-muted`, `--font-display`, `--text-body`, `--text-body-sm`, `--text-caption`, `--radius-md`, `--radius-lg`, `--motion-reveal`, `--ease-out`.

## Keyboard and screen reader

- **Nothing to operate,** so nothing takes focus.
- **It is a list named by `title`,** each track a heading and an ordered list of its steps. Each step says its name before its text, so the column headers, hidden from a screen reader, aren't needed to follow it.

## Content

- **Step names:** a few words, the same for every track: "How the price is set".
- **Bands:** one sentence each, ending in a full stop. Short enough for three lines at most.
- **Tracks:** two to five.
