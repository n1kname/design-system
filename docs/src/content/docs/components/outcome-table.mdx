## What it's for

To say what changed, one dimension at a time. For words, not figures.

## When not to use it

- **Figures you'd compare or sort.** Use a [data table](/components/data-table/).
- **A call to action.** The closing statement is a conclusion, so it's a tint rather than a solid fill.

## Anatomy

- **Dimension:** an icon and a name.
- **Outcome:** what changed, in a sentence.
- **Closing statement:** optional.

## Variants

None.

## States

It has no states of its own.

## Tokens

`--c-border`, `--c-surface`, `--c-surface-tint`, `--c-text`, `--c-text-accent`, `--font-display`, `--leading-h3`, `--radius-full`, `--radius-lg`, `--radius-md`, `--sp-2`, `--sp-3`, `--sp-4`, `--sp-5`, `--sp-6`, `--sp-7`, `--text-h3`, `--tracking-h3`. Needs `type.css`.

## Keyboard and screen reader

- **It's a description list,** so each dimension is read with its outcome. `headings` are for the eye, so they're hidden from screen readers.
- **The icons are hidden from screen readers.** Every row needs words that stand alone.

## Content

- **The row is the fact, not the icon.**
- **One claim per row.**

## Status

Used by the portfolio.
