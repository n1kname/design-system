---
title: Loading and empty
description: What a page does while it waits, when it has nothing, and when something fails.
---

The goal is a page that doesn't move. People lose their place when things jump, and a spinner tells them nothing they can use.

## While it waits

- **Reserve the space.** Every chart, stat and heading knows its height before its data arrives. Layout shift stays well below 0.1.
- **Under a second: nothing.** No spinner, no skeleton. The page holds its shape and the data appears.
- **No pulsing placeholders.** A looping animation asks for attention at the moment nothing can be done.
- **Over a few seconds: say so,** in words, in the place the data will go.
- **Settle in once.** Data fades in over `--motion-base` when it first arrives, and doesn't move on resize or refresh.

## When there's nothing

| What happened | What it says | Use |
|---|---|---|
| A value isn't known | A dash, never 0 | The cell or stat itself |
| Nothing exists yet | When it will | [Empty state](/components/empty-state/), `empty` |
| Nothing matches | That's the result, plus a way back | [Empty state](/components/empty-state/), `filtered` |
| The value is zero | 0 | The cell or stat itself |

Empty isn't zero. A chart with a gap draws a gap, not a line down to the axis.

## When it fails

- **Keep the last good figures,** and say how old they are.
- **Say what still works,** and offer one way to try again.
- **Use the `error` kind** of [empty state](/components/empty-state/), which is announced to screen readers.
