Name the role, not the colour. Every value comes from a warm neutral ramp and an orange accent ramp, computed in OKLCH so equal steps look equal.

The contrast shown is computed from the tokens at build time. WCAG 2.2 AA asks for 4.5:1 on text and 3:1 on graphics and control edges.

## Surfaces

The ratio here is against the page, and it's low on purpose. Surfaces separate through their borders.

## Borders

`border` is a decorative hairline, so no minimum applies. `border-strong` is for form controls and needs 3:1.
